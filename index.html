TVs and the price of the power they use

Three charts from Exercise 5: a vertical bar chart, a line chart with its data points, and a donut chart.

Which 55 inch screen type uses the most energy?

Screen size drives energy use, so this chart only compares 55 inch TVs, the median size in the dataset. LED models use the most on average, but the three screen types are within about 45 kWh of each other.

Mean energy consumption of 55 inch TVs by screen type. Source: Energy Rating TV dataset, January 2026.

How has the price of electricity changed?

The average wholesale spot price stayed between about $30 and $65 per megawatt hour until 2015, then rose sharply. It peaked in 2022.

Average of Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria and South Australia, 1998 to 2024. Tasmania and Snowy are excluded because they do not cover the full period. Source: ARE_Spot_Prices.csv.

How big are the TVs on sale?

Large TVs (65 inches or more) are the biggest group, and small TVs are only about one in eight models.

Small is under 40 inches, medium is 40 to 64 inches and large is 65 inches or more. Source: Energy Rating TV dataset, January 2026.
